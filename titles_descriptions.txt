SONG 01
TITLE: The Shape of Your Absence | Cinematic Emotional Pop

DESCRIPTION:
Some people leave our lives without leaving the world they changed behind.

A cup beside the window.
A familiar street beneath November rain.
A song that suddenly sounds different.
A silence that still feels like someone is there.

“The Shape of Your Absence” is a cinematic emotional pop ballad about the quiet traces love leaves behind after someone is gone.

With intimate female vocals, warm piano, atmospheric synths, subtle guitar textures, cinematic strings, and a slow-burning electronic pulse, the song moves from longing toward acceptance.

Sometimes letting go doesn't mean forgetting.
Sometimes it means carrying what was beautiful without carrying the pain.

🎧 Best experienced with headphones.

Genre: Cinematic Emotional Pop / Alternative Pop / Dream Pop
Mood: Romantic • Intimate • Melancholic • Warm • Hopeful
Vocals: Female • Soft • Breathy • Emotional
Tempo: 76 BPM
